Introduction to Vow.Foundation

Vow.Foundation claims to be a legitimate cryptocurrency project, but it has strong links to previous Ponzi schemes like The Last Network and VowCurrency.com.

Vow.Foundation and Its Connection to VowCurrency.com

VowCurrency.com had a notorious reputation for scamming investors. The website now redirects to Vow.Foundation, suggesting a direct link between the two. VowCurrency operated as a Ponzi scheme, promising high returns while relying on new investments to pay old investors.

Single.Id Scam - Another Fraudulent Scheme?

Single.Id is another entity connected to The Last Network and CashbackApp, both of which collapsed. These scams were operated under the UK shell company Enigmatic Smile Ltd and founded by Bish Smeir.

Bish Smeir - The Man Behind Multiple Ponzi Schemes

Bish Smeir is listed as one of the project owners on Vow.Foundation. His history includes running multiple collapsed Ponzi schemes, including The Last Network and CashbackApp.

Joerg D. Wittke’s Involvement in Vow.Foundation

Joerg Wittke, known for promoting BitClub Network, a $722 million Ponzi scheme, also promoted The Last Network, which has now rebranded into Vow.Foundation.

The $2 Billion VOW Token Exit Scam

On August 14, 2024, VOW claimed that a “bot” acquired and sold $2 billion worth of VOW tokens in a “market incident.” The so-called technical glitch caused the value of VOW to crash from 33 cents to $0.0028 in under 30 minutes.

"The bot acquired around 20 million VOW tokens from Uniswap and sent them to the contract resulting in nearly v$2 billion being created."

Conclusion: Is Vow.Foundation a Scam?

With direct links to collapsed Ponzi schemes, known scammers like Bish Smeir and Joerg Wittke, and a major exit scam in August 2024, Vow.Foundation is a scam. Similarly, Single.Id is another fraudulent scheme connected to past scams.
